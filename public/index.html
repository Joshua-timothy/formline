A model should explain itself.

Formline does not present probability as certainty. It makes the inputs and the limitations easier to see.

01 / TEAM STRENGTH

Opponent-adjusted ratings

Attack and defence ratings use time-decayed results, with stronger priors when little current-season evidence exists.

02 / MATCH CONTEXT

Form, rest and venue

Recent form is opponent-adjusted. Home advantage is regularised, while rest and congestion have bounded impact.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Calibration over hype

Forecast quality is shown in the dashboard. Every output is statistical information—not a promise or betting advice.
